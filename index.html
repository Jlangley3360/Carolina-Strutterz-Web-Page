About Carolina Strutterz

Our Story

What started as one team became something bigger before the first season ever began.

More Than Baseball

Developing players. Building character. Creating a place to belong.

What started as a plan for one team became something bigger than we ever imagined.

Carolina Strutterz Baseball was established in the fall of 2026 with one simple purpose: to create the kind of baseball experience we wanted for our own kids.

The plan was never to build a large organization. In fact, the plan was one team — one group of kids, one group of families, and an opportunity to build something special.

But before our first season even began, something incredible happened.People believed in the vision.

Families trusted us. Coaches stepped up. Players wanted to be part of it. And that one-team plan quickly became three teams in our very first season.

From the beginning, we’ve wanted Strutterz to be about more than wins, trophies, and weekends at the ballpark. We want to develop great baseball players, but we also want to build confidence, character, friendships, and a genuine love for the game.

We want every player who puts on a Strutterz jersey to know they are part of something that believes in them, challenges them, celebrates them, and gives them a place to belong.

Someday, the uniforms won’t fit anymore. The trophies will collect dust, and the final inning will be played. But the friendships, lessons, confidence, and memories made along the way can last a lifetime.

We started with plans for one baseball team.

We’re building something much bigger.

This is more than baseball. This is Carolina Strutterz. 🦃⚾

Meet the Founders

Tabitha & Eddie Whitman • Khristina & Bob Grant

📸

Founder Photo Coming Soon

Tabitha & Eddie Whitman • Khristina & Bob Grant

Before there was a Strutterz jersey, a roster, or a team on the field, there were two families with a shared vision for something different.

Throughout the fall of 2025 and spring of 2026, Tabitha and Eddie Whitman and Khristina and Bob Grant kept coming back to the same question:

“What if we created the kind of baseball organization we would want our own kids to grow up in?”

That question led to months of conversations, research, planning, and a whole lot of prayer.

They envisioned an organization where kids could compete and develop without losing their love for the game. Where coaches didn’t just see a player’s talent, but saw the kid underneath the jersey. Where families felt welcomed, players felt valued, and success was measured by more than a scoreboard.

Eventually, “What if?” became “Let’s do it.”

The four founders didn’t start Carolina Strutterz because they believed they had all the answers. They started it because they believed they could build something special — something they would be proud for their own children to be part of.

And that’s still the standard.

Because when a parent chooses Carolina Strutterz, they’re trusting us with far more than a baseball player.

They’re trusting us with their child.

We will never take that responsibility lightly.

Our founders may have started the Strutterz, but this organization belongs to every player, coach, and family who believes in what we’re building.

Our names may be behind the beginning.

But the kids will always be the reason. 🦃⚾

10U Whitman • Head Coach • Co-Founder

Eddie Whitman

Baseball has been a part of Eddie Whitman’s life for as long as he can remember.

Growing up with a love for the game, Eddie went on to play varsity baseball all four years of high school. Although his playing days eventually came to an end, his passion for baseball never did.

For the past five years, Eddie has coached youth baseball, dedicating his time to helping young athletes develop their skills, confidence, and understanding of the game.

As a coach, Eddie believes that developing a player starts with knowing the kid behind the jersey. He strives to build relationships with his players, earn their trust, challenge them to become better, and make sure they know their coach is always in their corner.

His approach is competitive, but his definition of success goes beyond wins and losses. Eddie wants his players to learn how to work hard, respond to adversity, be great teammates, compete with confidence, and never lose the love for the game that brought them to the field in the first place.

As Head Coach and Co-Founder of Carolina Strutterz Baseball, Eddie’s goal is to build teams that compete hard, develop the right way, and create an environment where young athletes can grow both on and off the field.

Because long after the scores are forgotten, players will remember the coaches who believed in them.

10U Whitman • Associate Head Coach • Co-Founder

Bob Grant

A lifelong baseball player and coach, Bob Grant brings years of playing experience, leadership, and a genuine passion for developing young athletes to Carolina Strutterz Baseball.

A 2007 graduate of Graham High School, Bob was a two-sport athlete for the Red Devils, competing in both baseball and football. After graduation, he continued his baseball career at North Carolina Central University, where he spent four years as part of the Eagles baseball program.

Bob returned to Graham High School as a baseball coach, serving from 2012–2020 and spending nearly a decade helping develop players both on and off the field.

From 2021–2023, Bob served as an assistant baseball coach at The Burlington School, where he was part of a program that captured back-to-back NCISAA State Championships.

From playing at the collegiate level to coaching championship baseball, Bob brings a wealth of knowledge and experience to the Strutterz. But what matters most to him isn’t simply developing better baseball players — it’s helping young athletes understand the game, believe in themselves, compete the right way, and become great teammates.

For Bob, coaching has always been about more than what happens between the lines.

It’s about using the game of baseball to make an impact that lasts long after the final out.

10U Whitman • Assistant Coach

Dylan Murray

Coach Dylan started playing baseball at the age of 4 and began playing travel baseball in middle school. He played for Western Alamance Middle School and continued on to play all four years of varsity baseball at Western Alamance High School.

Coach Dylan’s passion for coaching began when his oldest son started playing baseball. Since then, he has coached several seasons of T-ball, coach pitch, and now kid pitch, sharing his love and knowledge of the game with the next generation of players.

He takes pride in helping young players build a strong foundation through proper mechanics, confidence, discipline, teamwork, and a true love for the game. Most importantly, he wants his players to learn how to be not only better baseball players, but better teammates and young men. He loves being able to share a game that has been such a big part of his own life with the next generation and watching these players grow, both on and off the field.

In addition to his love for baseball, Coach Dylan is passionate about hunting, all things tractors and classic trucks, and most of all, spending time with his family.

For Coaches & Established Teams

Bring Your Team. Become Part of Something Bigger.

Already have a team you love? Bring them with you.

Carolina Strutterz Baseball is growing, and we’re looking for coaches and established teams who share our vision and want to become part of what we’re building.

We know how much time, work, and heart goes into building a team. You’ve developed relationships with your players. You’ve earned the trust of your families. You’ve created something you’re proud of.

We’re not asking you to leave that behind. We’re giving you the opportunity to bring it with you.

By joining the Carolina Strutterz organization, your team becomes part of a growing baseball family built around player development, competitive baseball, strong coaching, family, and putting kids first.

We’re looking for coaches who want to lead, develop players, build a positive team culture, and represent the Strutterz name with pride.

Whether you already have a complete roster or you’re building a team and looking for an organization to call home, we’d love to hear from you.

This isn’t about simply adding another team to our organization. It’s about finding the right people to grow it with us.
